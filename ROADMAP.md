## Done

### Video controls (0.5.0)

Instagram's web player has no way to skip forward or back in a video. InstaBasket adds a control bar over the video under the mouse (feed, post page, post popup, Reels): scrubber with buffered range and time, play / pause, 5 seconds back / forward and playback speed (0.5×–2×), plus ← / → and Space / K for the hovered video. See `extension/video.js`.
